## Why it's written this way (researched 2026-10-07)

- Instagram: sends per reach (DM shares) is the strongest signal for reaching non-followers, reported at 3 to 5 times the weight of likes (eclincher.com, posteverywhere.ai summaries of Mosseri). So the second line asks for a send to a specific person.
- Instagram search reads the caption, so the first line carries the search terms: money tips, credit freeze, IRS PIN, AI. Mosseri said in July 2026 that hashtags only help "ever so slightly on the margins" (kontentino.com), so the 5 tags are for topic context, not reach.
- TikTok indexes caption, on-screen text, speech and hashtags. Keyword in the first 100 characters, captions of 150 to 300 characters (capcut.com, trymypost.com). This description is about 230 characters.
- Both platforms cap hashtags at 5 (socialmediatoday.com, searchengineland.com for TikTok).
- TikTok photo carousels of 4 to 7 slides do best, and above 10 completion drops (ttcalculator.net). Option for TikTok: post slides 01, 02, 04, 05, 07, 10.
- Hashtag volumes were not measured (no access to platform data from this environment). The tags were picked for topic fit, not for post counts.
