## Proof: official source text (slides 02 to 06)

These slides quote the official pages listed in RESEARCH.md (URL, date checked, quote). They are shown as quote cards, clearly labeled "Quoted", not as fake screenshots.

Why not screenshots yet: this cloud session's egress proxy blocks irs.gov, labor.maryland.gov, chexsystems.com, nctue.com, innovis.com, lexisnexis.com, verizon.com, t-mobile.com and att.com, so no real browser capture was possible here. The quotes were verified through web search restricted to each primary domain.

To swap in real screenshots: run `node ~/.claude/skills/motion-carousel/engine/capture_sources.mjs <this folder>` on your Mac. It opens each URL in `proof.capture` (carousel.json), light mode, scrolls to the quoted sentence, and saves assets/sources/<slide>-<n>.png plus capture-log.json. Single-source slides (04 IP PIN, 06 Maryland) then render the real screenshot automatically on the next `render.mjs` run. A failed capture means the page wording changed, so re-check that quote before posting.
