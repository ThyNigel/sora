RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (Montgomery County, Maryland)
Landlord: Example Property Group LLC   Tenant: [REDACTED]   Premises: [REDACTED], Unit [REDACTED]

1. TERM. This Lease begins November 1, 2026 and ends October 31, 2027.
2. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,850.00, due on the 1st day of each month.
3. LATE FEE. If rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of 5% of the monthly rent.
4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,850.00. Landlord will return the deposit, less any lawful deductions, as required by Maryland law.
5. UTILITIES. Tenant pays electricity, gas, and internet. Landlord pays water and trash. A monthly common-area utility fee of $35.00 is charged with rent.
6. AMENITY AND ADMIN FEES. Tenant shall pay a one-time administrative fee of $150.00 at signing and a monthly amenity fee of $25.00.
7. PARKING. One reserved parking space is available for $60.00 per month.
8. PETS. Pets require written approval, a non-refundable pet fee of $300.00, and monthly pet rent of $40.00.
9. RENEWAL. This Lease converts to month-to-month unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end of the term. Month-to-month rent will be the current rent plus $150.00.
10. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may end this Lease early by giving 60 days written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.
11. REPAIRS. Tenant shall report needed repairs in writing through the resident portal. Tenant is responsible for repairs under $100.00 caused by ordinary use.
12. MOVE-OUT. Tenant shall give written notice of move-out at least 60 days before vacating. Carpet cleaning of $175.00 will be deducted from the security deposit at move-out.
13. RETURNED PAYMENTS. Any returned payment will be charged a fee of $35.00.
