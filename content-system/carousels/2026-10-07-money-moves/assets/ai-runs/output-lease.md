## 3. Ending the lease early

- **§10:** "Tenant may end this Lease early by giving 60 days written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two months' rent." ⚠
- That is roughly **$3,700**, plus 60 days' notice.
- The lease doesn't say whether you still owe rent during the 60 days. It also doesn't mention any special situations, such as military service or domestic violence. Maryland law has some protections for those, so ⚠ check them.

## 5. Security deposit

- **Amount:** $1,850.00 (one month's rent).
  - §4: "Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,850.00."
- **Return:**
  - §4: "Landlord will return the deposit, less any lawful deductions, as required by Maryland law."
  - §12: "Carpet cleaning of $175.00 will be deducted from the security deposit at move-out."
- ⚠ Check the **flat carpet-cleaning deduction** (regardless of the carpet's actual condition) and the **return deadline and itemization rules**. The lease defers to Maryland law and states no timeline of its own.

## Other items to double-check ⚠

- The **non-refundable $300 pet fee** and the **$150 admin fee**. Maryland has rules on what can be charged and how fees are disclosed.
- The **$150 month-to-month increase** (§9), including any required notice for rent changes.
- The **combined effect of recurring fees** ($35 + $25 on top of rent).
- §9 is ambiguous: it says the lease converts to month-to-month *unless* either party gives notice, but doesn't say what happens if notice *is* given (move out? new lease?).

## What I could not find in the lease

- Deadline for returning the deposit, interest on the deposit, move-in/move-out inspection rights, or an itemized deductions statement
- Landlord's repair obligations or response times, and emergency repair procedures
- Notice required before the landlord enters the unit
- Whether the late fee is one-time or recurs, and any grace period beyond the 5th
- Whether rent is owed during the 60-day early termination notice
- How written notice must be delivered (portal, mail, email) and to whom
- Rent increase notice rules for the lease term
- Subletting or assignment rules, holdover charges, and renter's insurance requirements
- Lead paint or other required disclosures, and any mention of the Tenants' Bill of Rights itself
- What counts as "ordinary use" or "lawful deductions"
- Pet approval criteria and what happens to pet rent if the pet leaves
- Whether the $35 common-area fee and $25 amenity fee can change during the term

This is a summary of what the document says, not legal advice. For the ⚠ items, Montgomery County's Office of Landlord-Tenant Affairs or a tenant-rights organization can confirm what applies.
